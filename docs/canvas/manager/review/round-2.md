## Cross-board fixes (do these first)

1. **Esther is Accounts, not Sales.** Two sheet heads give her the wrong role: MoneyCredit p4 (D-32) and DecideOrders p4 (D-19) both read "From Esther · Sales". BRIEF's people table and Main (D-19 "Accounts · propose", D-32 "Accounts · customer") say Accounts. Change both heads to **From Esther · Accounts**.
2. **Wall plugs cost 1,500, not 3,000.** These boards all price a pack at 1,500:
   - DecideStock p2: 199 × 1,500 = 298,500, and "Order only 1 · 1,500";
   - D-17: 40 → 44 = +6,000;
   - Main D-10: "2 packs … cost 3,000".

   DecideOrders p5 is the one that disagrees: it shows "At cost 2 × 3,000 = 6,000". Make it **2 × 1,500 = 3,000**.
3. **Check-in answer word.** §3 says the button = the asker's screen = **Take the check-in**. These places instead show "Ruth took Musa's check-in" as what Musa and Denis see:
   - DecideStock p3's dock;
   - DecideStock success;
   - the "Answers out" strip;
   - Main D-13's "asker's app" column.

   Use "Take the check-in" (or "Check-in taken" once it is added to §3 and the GLOSSARY). Do not use a third phrase.
4. **Invoiced vs Billed.** Orders uses two words for the same state. Pick the one in §3/GLOSSARY and use it on Orders and in Main's "asker's app" column.
   - The default push says "Grace sees: Invoiced", and Main D-8 and D-9 say "Invoiced".
   - The success push says "Billed · 567 · #0561".
5. **One red per screen.** Red should only ever be the FAB or one slide knob. These screens break that:
   - **Orders p4** shows the red FAB *and* the red knob on "Cancel #0545 · keep #0544". The Taken-twice slide belongs in a sheet, so give that row an **Open** pill, as Today does for slide decisions.
   - **Money p4** shows a filled navy "Limit 3,000,000 · 14 days" *and* a red merge slide in the dock. That makes two primary actions. Keep the limit answer as the primary on this screen, and move the merge to the Look-alike card's **Open**.
   - **DecideOrders p4**: the "Void" twin tile is dashed in accent red and has a red "Void" tag, next to the red knob. Draw the twin in `--bad` grey-red or plain navy outline.
   - **Orders p1, Orders p3, DecideStock p1 and Team p3** still stack several over-limit reds on one screen (red-tinted column, red border, red bar, red figures). They are `--bad`, not accent, but there are too many. Keep one over-limit mark per card: the bar's over part. Put the figures in ink.
6. **Offline words differ by board.** Each board uses its own word. Pick one tail, from the GLOSSARY, and use it on all.

   | Board | Offline word |

   | Today | "Waits" |
   | Orders | "Waiting · Nudge Denis" |

The Agent ChangeAsk/MoveSent sheets pull their action up under the content. Either raise the dock, or fill the gap with the next-decision card, as DecideOrders p1/p3 do.
