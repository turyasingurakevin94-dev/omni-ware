# FACTS: the one sample day for the Sales redesign

Every board on every tab (Today, Chats, Sell, Counter, Orders, Customers) draws this day. Where a board disagrees, the board changes (see `FACTS-diff.md`).

**Precedence used to pick each value:**
1. The published language: `Main.dc.html`, `Main2.dc.html` and the sample numbers in `LANGUAGE.md`.
2. Then by topic:
   - money owed and customer rhythm: Customers;
   - orders (numbers, times, totals, stages, lines): Orders;
   - counts on Today, the tab-bar badges and the chats waiting: Today.
3. Otherwise the majority of boards.
4. Item spelling is a word choice, so it follows the majority of boards.

Two rules hold everything together:
- **Last order = the customer's most recent order placed.** Next due = last order + their rhythm. A customer who ordered today is never shown as due.
- **One "now".** See §1 for the few boards that show another moment.
