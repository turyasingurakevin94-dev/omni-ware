The three states a screen is only designed once you have drawn them.

1 · Nothing on the book yetA new shop, or a shop that has never written a customer down. No strip of zeros: four figures reading 0 are four figures that mean nothing.

The booknobody on file

Nobody is on the book yet.

Everything this screen shows — what each customer is worth, how often they come, what they owe — is read from your invoiced orders and the debt book. So the book fills itself as you sell: invoicing a name that is not on file offers to add them there and then, and you never have to type anyone in twice.

If you are moving an existing shop in, start with the people who owe you money — an opening balance carries the date of its oldest charge, so the ageing on Debtors is right from the first day rather than showing every old balance as new.

2 · On the book, never invoicedThree customers in this shop carry a charged-and-settled history against invoices that were never linked to their record. Zeros would call that nothing; dashes and a sentence call it what it is.

Nalongo EnterprisesWakiso · C021 · no number on file 203 days past terms — — — — 1,240,000

Nalongo Enterprises has no invoiced orders yet — so what they are worth, how often they come and what you keep on them cannot be worked out, and are shown as dashes rather than zeros. Those figures appear the day their first order is billed.

What is known is the debt book: 1,240,000 charged by hand on 14 February 2026 as an opening balance, and nothing paid against it since. That is 203 days, and there is no phone number on the record to ask on.
