How the line is worked out

It starts at what the cash book says you hold this morning and steps down on each day something you have already promised falls due. It is a step, never a slope: money does not drain away between payments, it sits still and then goes.

Safe to spend is the lowest point the line reaches, not what it ends on. Ending the month at 110,000 is no comfort if you are under water on the 24th, and What to buy budgets from this figure.

What a date does

A bill with a day on it steps the line down on that day, and safe to spend drops by it. One with no day stays off the line entirely — until somebody says a date, putting it there would mean inventing one.

Nothing here is a guess. Wages a month nobody has counted the days for are named as missing rather than estimated, and a bill nobody has dated is counted below, not above.
