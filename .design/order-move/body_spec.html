The button is never one that refuses

A stage that cannot be left yet does not offer to leave it. The card carries the act that CAN be done now — which is always the thing standing in the way — and only becomes the move once nothing is. Nothing is greyed out, nothing pops up to say no, and there is no toast to read.

After that, the ··· beside the act holds every move that is not the next one — back a stage, on hold, or cancelled. It sits in the same place on every card, so there is no second button to look for and none to press by accident.
