How the order is worked out

What a payment does

It clears that supplier's oldest bill first, the way a ledger settles, and posts straight to the cash book as a stock purchase. Part payments are fine; whatever is left keeps its original age rather than starting again.

Naming a day

A bill with a day on it goes onto the cash line in What's coming, and what you can safely spend drops by it. One with no day stays off that line — until somebody says a date, putting it there would mean inventing one.
