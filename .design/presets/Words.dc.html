The words the shop uses

Typed into this shop’s own forms. Nothing here moves a figure — a list only decides what a field suggests, and keeps one thing spelled one way.

Three words are in two places at once, and one category is holding everything.

Hinges, Wall Plugs and Channels each appear twice. Hinges is a category AND a sub-category of Furniture; Wall Plugs sits under both Furniture and Gypsum; Channels under both Gypsum and Aluminum. A product filed under one of them cannot be found by the other.

Furniture holds 39 of the shop’s 64 sub-categories, and at least 21 of those are tools or consumables. It has become the place a word goes when nobody picks a category.

8 of 17 categories have nothing under them, and 5 of those name something that already exists inside Furniture — Handles, Locks, Hinges, Runners and Curtain Fittings.

The panel header counts 139 because that is what the six lists hold. It is not 139 + 64: a sub-category belongs to its category, and counting it twice would make the shop’s vocabulary look half again as large as it is.

What the band will not say

It will not call a word wrong. Whether a bow saw belongs under Furniture is the shop’s judgement, not the app’s. What it reports is only what it can prove from the lists themselves — the same word twice, a category with nothing under it, a name that already exists inside another.

And it goes quiet when the lists are clean. No duplicates, nothing empty, no catch-all: no band. Not a tick, not “0 issues” — nothing at all, the way the unset band above it already behaves.
