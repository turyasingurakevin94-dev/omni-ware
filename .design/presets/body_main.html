The rules it works by

Every number the app assumes, and the screen each one drives. Five of them are set on the screen that uses them; they are listed here so this page is the whole answer.
Set here 6 rules
Default price rule

The selling rule for a product that has none of its own. Percent adds on top of the item’s cheapest supplier cost; fixed adds a flat amount, per pack wholesale and per piece retail. A rule on the product always wins.

Unset. 23 lines fall through to this and find nothing. They cannot be quoted, sold, or catalogued until either they get a rule of their own or this one is set.

A share of what you make on an item, not off its price. 50% gives an agent half your margin; 100% gives all of it and they pay your cost exactly. Below cost is not reachable whatever is set here. Any product can override it.

Who can change what

Everything here saves the moment you leave a field — one grammar for the whole page, no Save button, and no draft to lose. The one exception is going live on mobile money, which asks first, because it starts moving real money.

Counter and picker logins see the words and nothing else. Admins see the words and the rules. Owners see all three regions. A locked region is shown locked rather than hidden, so nobody concludes the shop cannot take mobile money.
