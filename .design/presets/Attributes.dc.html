The two groups are the whole design. 22 attributes are on a product; 36 are on none. That is not a judgement about which are good — a shop can add an attribute the day before it builds the variants that use it — but it is the one fact that lets 58 be read at all, and it is derivable from the products themselves rather than typed here.

What the split shows

colour, Color and Colour are three attributes. Only one is on a product. Case and spelling made three where the shop meant one — the same fault as Hinges next door, and it is why the list reached 58.

Four have no values at all. A name was typed into a variant form and nothing followed it. They suggest nothing and can never match anything.

Nothing is deleted for you. The screen sorts and counts; what to prune is the shop’s call. The same Merge sheet works here — merging colour into Colour moves its values across.
