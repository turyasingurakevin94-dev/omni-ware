Your margin

What every line you sold actually kept, against what the rule pricing it says it should. The two are different questions: a gap between them is a line being sold off its own rule, and a rule that yields almost nothing is a rule that has gone stale — a fixed markup of five thousand was a fair mark on a ten thousand item and is a rounding error on a hundred thousand one, and nothing here had ever divided the one by the other. Ranked by what lifting each to your target would have added over the units that really sold, so the work starts at your biggest sellers. Nothing changes until you tap it and confirm it.

Clearance

Everything on your shelf that has not sold inside the quiet window, worst money first, with what it cost you and how long it has sat. Set a price you would take for it and the screen says plainly what that means — including when it is below what you paid, which is your call to make and not the app’s. Underneath each line are the customers who have actually bought it before, most first, each with an offer already written naming only the lines they have a history with. Read it, change it, send it with your own thumb — the app never sends anything by itself.
