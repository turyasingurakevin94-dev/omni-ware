How the order is worked out

Two kinds of money sit on one list, so they are ranked on one clock: what a fix is worth over a year. A thin line's month is counted twelve times; dead stock's cash comes back once.

That is why +529,000 a month outranks 1,950,000 sitting on a shelf. Each row still shows the honest figure — a month, or once — and nothing on this screen ever adds the two together.
