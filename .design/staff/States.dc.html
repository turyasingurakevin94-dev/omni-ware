The staff row, in every state

One person, one row, one status. The old screen carried five figures on every row and a column of em-dashes for anyone the figure did not apply to — which reads as a bad score rather than as a question that does not apply. Here the row carries only what changes whether you hand this person the next order; the rest opens underneath.

Status — the four, and no fifth
Free In today, signed in, holding nothing. Verdigris, because on this screen free is the good answer.
Carrying 2 Working. Plain steel — a busy person is not a problem, and colour here would mark everything.
Absent today Amber. Something to attend to today, and it costs money — the row says how much.
Unavailable Crimson, and the row greys. They are off the dispatch list until you put them back.

“No app login” is amber and sits in the status column too, because it answers the same question the column exists for: can this person be handed an order right now? They cannot.
