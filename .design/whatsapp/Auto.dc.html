Read the last column carefully. 13% of the system’s answers became orders against 30% of yours, and that is not evidence it answers worse. It only ever takes the questions where a customer named a product outright — often a price check at night, from someone who was never going to order that week. You take the hard ones, which are hard because somebody is deciding. The two columns are not the same question.

What the arithmetic does support: 9 questions got no answer at all, and the median person waited 37 minutes for you. That is the cost the system is against, and it is the number worth watching.

Widening it a dry run, nothing sent

Today the system answers only when a customer says a product’s whole name and nothing belonging to another. Letting it answer when they said most of the name would have covered 22 more questions this month. Here is what it would have said, on the real messages, without sending anything.

“how much for 30 iron sheets 28 gauge?” Would have said: Iron Sheets (28 gauge, plain), 48,000 a sheet; 30 comes to 1,440,000. You said the same thing 41 minutes later.
“mabati price?” Would have said nothing — mabati is not a word it knows yet. It is waiting for you under Words your customers use.
“do you have cement and nails” Would have said nothing. Two products in one question is a conversation, and a conversation is yours.
