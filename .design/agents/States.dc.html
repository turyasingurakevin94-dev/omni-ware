Sales agents

Who is holding your stock, what they owe you, and what you owe them.

1 · Nobody has been invited yet

You have no sales agents.

An agent is somebody outside the shop who sells your stock through the Agent app, at your prices, and keeps their own margin on top. Their clients stay private to them — you see the order, never the buyer.

Inviting one sends an email. They pick their own password; you never see it. Their Agent ID is issued by the server, not typed here, so it can never collide with an ID that has history behind it.

Invite your first agent
Before you do

Decide the terms first. Pay before we prepare means nothing leaves the shop until the money is in. Pay on delivery means goods go out on trust, and whatever is not paid for is yours to chase from somebody who does not work for you. Every agent starts on prepay, and moving one off it asks you to confirm with what they already owe on the screen.
