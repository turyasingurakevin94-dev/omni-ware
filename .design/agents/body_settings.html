Why this moved off Sales agents

Merchant API keys, a sandbox switch and a callback URL are not a view of your agents — they are set up once, changed almost never, and read only by an owner. Sitting them behind a fourth sub-tab on the roster made the agents screen four unrelated screens wearing one name, and put the shop’s live payment credentials one tap away from the list you open every morning.
