When there is no photograph
A dashed rule on the pale ground, and the words “No photograph yet”. Never a grey rectangle pretending to be an image, and never a stock picture of something similar.

Where it matters — a colour, a finish, a make — it offers the act: ask, and the shop sends one on WhatsApp.
Where photographs never go
The statement. The receipt. Your account. The storekeeper's count.

Photographs belong to identity — deciding which thing you mean. They have no business on a screen about money, where they would only make a figure harder to find. That line is what keeps this from becoming a shop with a ledger bolted on.
