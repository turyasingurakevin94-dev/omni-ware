“You last paid”

The highest price you have paid this supplier for that same line, on a bill that was not voided, with the day you paid it. Where you have never bought the line from them, the column reads first time and nothing is flagged — a price with nothing to compare it to is not a fault.

Prices are only ever compared for the same unit. A keg against a kilo is not a rise.

Correcting a bill

Money only. Quantities, the shelf and the stock record are untouched — if what arrived was not what was billed, that is Undo the delivery, and it is a different act with different consequences.

Every correction keeps the original figures on the record, so a bill you argued down still shows what was first asked.
