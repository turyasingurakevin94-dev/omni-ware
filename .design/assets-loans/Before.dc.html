What is wrong with them
1
Two screens for one question.The van is on Assets. The loan that bought the van is on Loans. Neither page mentions the other, so “what is this thing actually worth to me” cannot be asked anywhere.
2
The accent is on filing, not on the work.Both pages spend their one red button on Add an asset and Record a loan — things done twice a year. A repayment two months overdue gets no accent on either page.
3
Nothing is ranked.Both tables sort by the date the thing was acquired or taken. The loan in arrears sits wherever its start date puts it; here that is the top by luck, not by rule.
4
The reading that matters is three clicks deep.Worth against owed — the whole point of a thing bought on credit, and the app already computes it — lives inside the depreciation-schedule modal, behind an unlabelled icon.
5
Seven columns at one weight, then four unnamed icons.Every column is set in the same ink at the same size, and the last cell is four icon buttons with no column name, one of which deletes the record.
6
A paragraph nobody reads twice, permanently unfolded.Forty-five words of teaching sit above the work every single visit. The console folds that behind the “i” and keeps one line on screen.
