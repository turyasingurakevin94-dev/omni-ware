What moves, and what does not
1
The two rail entries become one.Assets and Loans go; Assets & loans takes their place in Money. Both old keyword sets are kept on it, so searching “depreciation” or “borrowed” still lands here.
2
The performance modal and the schedule modal go.Everything in both is on the opened row instead. The add and edit forms stay modals — a form is not a reading, and opening one in a list would push the list about.
3
The daily due reminder stays where it is.It fires on Today and it is the right place for it. The register no longer needs its own banner back into it, because what is behind is now the first row of the page.
4
Nothing was deleted from the arithmetic.Depreciation, effective rates on flat loans, fees withheld, disposal gains, the equity schedule — all still derived from the same records. The redesign moves where they are read, not what they say.
