Taken 1

today: Draft

A saved quote is an order. The app numbers it, splits lines into own shelf and to buy in, and lists which suppliers to ask.

What moves it

Every supplier on it says yes to the terms. yours

Your taps
  • Ask — one tap per supplier opens their WhatsApp with their lines already written
  • Confirmed — one tap per answer, on the row, not in a pop-up

Buying 2

today: Awaiting Goods

The order leaves Taken by itself on the last answer. The pickers’ phones are told. Its bought-in lines join the next buying trip, planned by place.

What moves it

The last line checked in on the buying list, back from the trip. itself, as now

Your taps
  • Announce to the sales group — one tap, copy and paste, never a gate
  • Decide a short delivery: amend, or keep the rest on the next trip

Preparing 3

today: Being Prepared

When a picker finishes, their phone hands them the order that has waited longest. The board shows who has it and how far.

What moves it

The picker finishes on their phone. the picker’s tap

Your taps
  • None. The assign pop-up goes: a picker is not a decision the desk has any basis to make
  • Decide a short pick, if one is recorded

Out for delivery 4

today: Pending Delivery

Transport is hired, so nobody on staff sees the drop. The picker taps Loaded with the carrier’s name and phone — a note on the order, not a gate.

What moves it

The customer or the carrier says it arrived, by phone. yours, one tap

Your taps
  • Delivered — on the row, when the call comes
  • An agent’s own pickup: Collected, as now

Delivered 5

today: Completed

The row shows the check: lines picked against ordered, the total, the customer’s credit. Invoice is the next tap on the same row.

What moves it

You invoice: stock out, supplier bills raised, the debt on the account. yours

Your taps
  • Invoice — one tap, or Invoice all delivered at the end of the day
  • Then Chase debts takes the money, as now

Stays yours, always
  • Saying a supplier’s terms are agreed
  • Deciding a short delivery or a short pick
  • Saying it arrived
  • Invoicing, and cancelling — now on the board, with the bought goods going to the shelf
The app never
  • Messages a customer about an order — your choice, kept
  • Invoices by itself, or moves an order to Delivered
  • Picks a picker for you: the pickers’ phones hand out, and the board says who
Honest limits
  • WhatsApp gives an app no way into a group and opens one chat at a time, so asking is one tap per supplier and the group message stays a copy and a paste — one tap, never a gate
  • Alerts reach you only while the app is open; the pickers’ phones get push
  • Hired transport has no app, so Delivered is a phone call away, as now
