The one line that makes it work. The row’s click handler must start if(e.target.closest(’.img-zoomable’)) return; — without it the row toggle fires as well and shuts the row underneath the lightbox, so the picture appears and vanishes. index.html already does exactly this at line 74072 for Sourcing’s open-row photo. And the honest cost: the row is two targets now. A press meant for the line that lands in the left 44px opens a picture instead. That is the trade, and it is why the glyph is on at rest rather than on hover.
