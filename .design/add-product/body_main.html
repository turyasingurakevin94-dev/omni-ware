Proposed — step one of one

Same modal shell, same controls, same tokens. What changed is what the screen claims to be: a form for naming a thing, sized to the thing it names.

Down the form
1The ID is a fact, not a field. It moves into the head as P014 — read-only ink where read-only information belongs — and the dead disabled input is gone. One less control, and the thing it withheld is now visible before you save.
2One sentence, unfolded. Replaces three "i" bubbles, and says the thing the old form never said: a product with no price cannot be quoted. Measure-limited, so it can never run 180 characters wide.
3The required field is the biggest control on the screen — full width, 42px, and wide enough for "Iron sheets — G28, 3m box profile" without clipping. The grid is declared, not automatic, so nothing reflows into four equal columns again.
Colour and consequence
4The accent appears once. Selected "Sold as" is steel — an inset rule and a filled dot, a shape change rather than a colour one. Oxide is reserved for Save, which is the one thing to do next.
5The choice carries its own consequence. "Sizes or options — you build them next" tells you a second step exists before you commit to it, and the button below changes its words to match.
What was taken away
6The rail is gone. A four-door rail for a form with one door was the original fault. Photo, markup and agent share sit behind one closed row that says what is set in it — so nothing is hidden, and nothing is in the way. 6 controls on open instead of 14.
7The footer says what saving does and does not do. The app's law is that nothing sends itself; this is the screen saying so. On an invalid form this same line names the missing field in crimson and Save goes flat — no toast 900px away from the fault.
Unchanged on purpose
·The modal shell, the .btn family, .field ink and the Required chip are the app's own — no fourteenth button family, no twenty-fourth colour, no new font size off the ramp.
