As it stands

The register's front door, at 1440. Eight faults, and the first three are the same fault: the form does not know which of its fields matters.

Weight in the wrong place
1Four sections for a form with one. Photo, Markup and Agent terms are almost never filled at add time — yet they take three of the four doors, and each opens onto a panel with two fields in it.
2The one required field is the narrowest control on the screen. auto-fit minmax(160px,1fr) gives every field the same 162px, so "Wheelbarrow, heavy duty" shows as "Wheelbarrow, h" — and an <input> cannot ellipsis, so there is no mark that anything was cut.
3A dead box that looks fillable. Product ID is disabled and empty. It is not a field — it is a fact issued on save — and it is sitting in the second most prominent slot in the form.
4Two fields deep, six fields wide, no order. The grid is automatic, so nothing was decided: the label "Product name" wraps to two lines and drops its input 17px below its neighbours', and the row baselines stop lining up.
Colour that means nothing
5The accent appears four times. Selected type card, the Required chip's amber, the active tab, and Save. The house rule is once per screen — it is the one thing to do next. Four accents point nowhere.
6Save is live on an invalid form. Press it empty and you get a toast in the far corner, 900px from the field at fault, which disappears on its own.
Said twice, or not at all
7"Basics" is written twice — once in the rail, once as the heading beside it — and three "i" bubbles hide the sentences that would have told you what the form is for.
8Nothing says what a saved product still cannot do. A product with no price cannot be quoted or sold. The form ends on "Save product" as though that were the finish line.
And behind the fifth door
9Variants are stacked cards, 410px each. A SKU, a photo picker and two markup overrides that are nearly always blank. Twenty-four variants is 9,800px of scrolling inside a modal.
