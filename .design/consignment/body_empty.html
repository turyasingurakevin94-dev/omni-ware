Consignment

i

Goods on your shelf that are not yours, and what has sold of them

This screen cannot record anything yet. Consignment needs one update to your database — somewhere to store which supplier owns each delivery. Until it is applied, receiving on consignment is refused rather than accepted and lost.
alter table stock_lots add column if not exists consign text;
Send this to whoever manages the database, or paste it into the Supabase SQL editor. It takes effect within half a minute, without reloading.
Consignorsnone

Nothing is held on consignment.

When a supplier leaves goods to be paid for as they sell, receive them on the Inventory screen and tick on consignment. No bill is raised then — what you owe builds here as they sell, and you settle when they come round.

Both are refused until the database update above has been applied.
