What settling does

It raises an ordinary supplier bill for what has sold. The money then reaches Who you owe, the payables and the cash book by the route every other bill takes.

Nothing is paid by settling. You pay that bill the usual way, in full or in part.

Unsold goods can go back at any time, owing nothing.
