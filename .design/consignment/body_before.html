Consignment

Goods a supplier has left with you to sell and be paid for as they sell. They sit on your shelf and can be sold like anything else — but they are not yours, so they are not counted in what your stock is worth, and the moment one sells, part of the money in the drawer belongs to whoever left it. That running figure is what this screen exists for. Settling raises an ordinary supplier bill for what has sold, which you then pay the usual way; unsold goods can go back at any time, owing nothing.
