Where a correction lives

Today it lives wherever the thing being corrected happens to be shown, so putting one mistake right means first guessing which screen owns it.

And nowhere can you say “this never happened.” Correcting a purchase refuses a quantity of nothing, so a delivery entered by mistake has to be disguised as a smaller delivery. That is how one wrong row became three.

Proposed — one door, from wherever you noticed

Put it right is anchored on the movement, because a movement is the thing that was recorded wrong and this log is the only screen that shows it in the order it happened. Inventory and Purchase invoices keep one button each, and all three land here.
