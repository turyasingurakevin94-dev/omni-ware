Follow-ups

Everyone with a reason to hear from you, in one queue, one message each. Ring them, take a payment, write down a day they promised, add to their list — all from here. The app never sends anything by itself.

What puts a client in the queue

A day you said you would get back by. Money past your terms that Chase would ask for today. Goods back on the shelf, a sourcing ask that has moved on, a price off the figure they were quoted. Something worth telling them. Or a silence past your Quiet after setting. One reason each, the dearest first, so nobody is listed twice.

Who is held back, and why

Anyone told inside the last fortnight, chased in the last week, past your terms or with no number on file. Nobody chased for money also gets a picture of cement — they get the statement. They are named in Held back rather than dropped.

The picture

Something worth telling goes as a picture built from their own dealings: what they buy, how many they take, what they pay. One per client per fortnight. About twenty words reach the client; the whole reasoning stays on this side of the glass. Share opens WhatsApp with the picture attached and their chat chosen, you press send, and only then is it written down as told.

Whether any of it works

“Bought within 14 days” is not proof the message did it — somebody due cement would have bought cement anyway. It is the only measure the books can honestly give, and a reason that never converts sinks down the ranking on its own.

Money is not counted, and cannot be. A chase is written down against the client, and that note is dropped the moment they owe nothing — which is exactly when the chase worked. Any rate counted from it would be wrong in your own favour, so it is not counted at all. What a chase actually did is on that client’s own statement, payment by payment.

Tellings about goods still being sourced are left out of the rate too: there is nothing on the shelf for them to buy yet, so counting them would libel the reason.

The rules are the whole algorithm

There is no model behind them. At 116 customers the rules are more accurate than anything trained, and you can read why every row is here. Companion quantities come from rules you wrote under What goes with what; only those carrying a figure can put a quantity on a picture. A site stage is asked once and goes stale by itself, and it is set on the client’s own record.
