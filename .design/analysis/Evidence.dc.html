How this is ordered

Two kinds of money are on this list and nothing here ever adds them. A margin you lift comes back every month. Cash you are owed, or cash locked in stock, comes back once.

To put them in one order, everything is counted over a year — a month’s money twelve times, a one-off once. That is why 985,600 a month sits above 2,480,000 in Kato’s account. Every row says which kind it is, so you can disagree with the order.
