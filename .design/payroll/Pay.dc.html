How the order is worked out

Anything already late first, oldest first. Then whatever falls due soonest — wages all land on the last day of the month, so they arrive together. Within one day, the largest month first, because that is the one today's cash decides.

A month that cannot be costed sits below the payable ones: there is no figure to pay, so it cannot be ranked against money. Settled months stay on the list — they are the record that August was paid — at the foot, at full strength.

What paying does

You choose the account. The money leaves cash, mobile money or bank and is filed in the Cash Book under Salaries & Wages or Rent, dated today — the same two categories the shop always used.

Part payments are ordinary: pay a fortnight now and the month keeps the rest owed. A wage settled before you started using this system can be marked as such, and the Cash Book is left exactly as it stands.

What is owed here is on the balance sheet as a liability the day it is earned — the profit statement still counts it when the money moves. Rent agreements · Staff
