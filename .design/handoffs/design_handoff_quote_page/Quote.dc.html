Why Saved quotes is not a screen of its own any more
A quote being built and a quote saved are one document at two moments — the same lines, the same client, the same arithmetic, and the same file. Two rail rows meant a quote had two homes and neither was the real one, and the saved list could not be reached from the screen where a quote is written. They are two lenses over one screen now, the pattern this app already uses for Invoices (sales / bills) and Inventory (shelf / movements).
· What is lost: nothing. Every saved quote is reachable in one tap from the screen that writes quotes.
· What absorbs it: the Quote row, whose rail count becomes the saved total (8) rather than a second row's badge.
· The words still reach it: "saved", "drafts", "pending", "waiting", "estimate", "proposal" move onto Quote's keywords, and resolveTab('quote-saved') keeps the old door open on the Saved lens.
