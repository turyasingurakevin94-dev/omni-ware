The cut: three screens about price become one
The rail draws the final map: Sell 6, Buy 2, Catalogue 4, Money 4, Insight 4, Setup 3 — 23 rows and Today, from 33 and Today. Every cut is argued in Rail.dc.html, which is the authority if a frame disagrees.
Three rows asked one question. Price registry held what you paid, the tiers and the rival prices; Pricing, over in Money, held the markup rules with thin lines and dead stock; Compare prices, over in Buy, ranked suppliers with no quantity attached. Neither name said which screen owned a markup, they sat in three different groups, and that split is what let a product show a wholesale markup in one place while the quote line beside it sold at cost. One row now, in Catalogue, beside the things being priced.
What is lost
Two rail rows and three filter bars. Nothing in the content: rules, what you paid, tiers, rivals, thin lines and dead stock are all lenses here, over one list and one search.
What absorbs it
This screen. Comparing suppliers happens on the line’s panel, where the quantity is known — and price is tiered, so a comparison without one compares a figure nobody is charged. Photographs went the other way, onto Products, since a picture is a field on the record.
The words still reach it
"registry", "markup", "margin", "rule", "what we paid", "compare", "cheapest", "rivals", "thin" and "dead stock" all become Pricing keywords, each old door resolving to the lens it meant.
Three things this screen says that no figure in the app said before: a side with no rule sells at cost — 14 lines are one loose sale away from it, and three ELEPHANT cartons already went out at 300,000 with a 10,000 markup sitting on file. A fixed wholesale markup is per pack, so the per-unit figure is printed beside it: +6,000 on a carton of twelve is +500 a tin, and the same number read the wrong way is twelve times the price. And inheritance is drawn, not hidden — a grey figure means the line is using the product's or the supplier-book's rule, a coloured one means it sets its own, so the G28 · 3m variant's 10% is visible as the exception it is. The fuller argument, and the other seven rows that go, are in Rail.dc.html.
