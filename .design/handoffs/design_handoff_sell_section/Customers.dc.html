The cut: Debtors becomes the Owing lens
The rail draws the final map: Sell 6, Buy 2, Catalogue 4, Money 4, Insight 4, Setup 3 — 23 rows and Today, from 33 and Today. Every cut is argued in Rail.dc.html, which is the authority if a frame disagrees.
Last turn I left Debtors standing because chasing is a different job from reading. Building this screen changed my mind. A debtor is a customer with a balance — the same record, sorted differently — and the code says so: renderCustomers() and renderDebtorsList() have to be called together after every void or payment, and when one of them is missed the two screens disagree about the same debt. That is not two jobs, it is one list with two renderers and a synchronisation bug waiting.
What is lost
Nothing. Debtors ranked by amount alone; the Owing lens ranks by age then amount, which is the order a shop actually asks in, and keeps the aging bar Debtors had at the top.
What absorbs it
This screen's Owing lens, on by default when the rail badge is non-zero. One record, one balance, one reckoning — the drift check moves onto the customer panel where it can be seen.
The words still reach it
"debtors", "who owes me", "aging", "receivables" and "credit" become Customers keywords, and resolveTab('debtors') opens Customers with the Owing lens armed.
Two insights this screen adds that the data already held: margin per customer against the shop average — Mulongo buys the thinnest thing you sell, so the biggest account is not the best one — and the reason for the quiet: their buying stopped the month their invoice fell due, which reads as a credit block, not lost interest.
