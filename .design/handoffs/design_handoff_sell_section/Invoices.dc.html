The cut: Statements stops being a screen
The rail draws the final map: Sell 6, Buy 2, Catalogue 4, Money 4, Insight 4, Setup 3 — 23 rows and Today, from 33 and Today. Every cut is argued in Rail.dc.html, which is the authority if a frame disagrees.
A statement is not a place, it is a document produced from rows this screen already holds — one customer's invoices, the payments against them, and the balance that falls out. Statements re-drew that list under its own filter bar, so the same figures were reckoned twice from two date ranges, and a shop reading a customer's account had to choose which screen to trust.
What is lost
Nothing readable. The account view moves into the invoice panel (This customer's account) and onto the customer's own record.
What absorbs it
Send statement becomes an action on a customer — print, PDF or WhatsApp — beside the existing client copy. One reckoning, the register's.
The words still reach it
"statement", "account", "running balance", "ledger" and "as at" move onto Customers' keywords, and resolveTab('statements') opens the customer picker with the statement action armed.
Two more worth arguing after this screen: the order-stage board (quoted / packing / out) is a third lens of this register rather than its own screen — an order is an invoice before it is paid. And Debtors is this screen's overdue group ranked by who to ask first; it keeps its row only because chasing is a different job from reading, which is a judgement, not a fact.
