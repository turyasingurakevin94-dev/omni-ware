1 — Nothing on the book yet

No suppliers on file yet

There is no strip and no ranking above this, because four figures reading zero are four figures that mean nothing. A supplier here is a means to an end: until a price is entered against them in the Price book they cannot be picked as a source for anything, so adding a name on its own changes nothing you can act on.

If you already owe somebody from before you started using this, put that in when you add them — it becomes a purchase invoice dated to the charge it came from, which is why Creditors ages it correctly from day one instead of showing an old balance as new.
