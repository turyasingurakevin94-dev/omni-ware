Privacy Policy

Omni-ware hardware shop · last updated 6 August 2026

This page explains what information we handle when you contact our shop or buy from us, including when you message us on WhatsApp or browse our online catalogue.

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name and phone number, when you message us, place an order, or are recorded as a customer of the shop.
  • Messages — the content of conversations you have with the shop on WhatsApp, so we can answer your questions and fulfil your orders.
  • Order history — what you have bought or asked us to quote, so we can serve you and keep honest accounts.

What we use it for

  • Replying to your enquiries and quotations.
  • Preparing, delivering and invoicing your orders.
  • Keeping the shop's own sales and stock records.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We do not send you marketing messages unless you have asked for them, and you can opt out at any time by telling us so in a message.

Where it is kept

Our records are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and are accessible only to the shop's own staff. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (except records we must keep for accounting). Just send us a WhatsApp message or speak to us at the shop.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the shop directly — message us on WhatsApp on the number you already use to reach us, or visit us in person.
